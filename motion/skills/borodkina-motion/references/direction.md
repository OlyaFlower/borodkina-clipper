# Motion direction for Olya Borodkina covers (distilled)

Source: the brand's motion-direction guide (motion-engine `docs/motion-direction/README.md` and
`living-scenes.md`), adapted to an 8 s loop that starts and ends on the cover.

## Idea first

Say in one sentence what the viewer sees and why they keep watching, without "premium", "cinematic" or
"cool". Choose **one main visual event**; the rest prepares it, supports it or resolves it. For each beat,
state what changes and what the eye should notice first. A beat without a purpose goes.

## A world, not a decorated picture

- Three depth roles: foreground (proximity, overlap), midground (the main action), background (space).
  The parallax gives them different motion; effects belong to one of them.
- Detail needs a physical reason: dust floats in light, beams come from lamps, rays from the sun, glow from
  highlights, hatching follows tone. Random particles are not a substitute for a reason.
- Quiet regions guide attention. Keep one dominant focal action; others support through scale, tone and
  direction.
- At thumbnail size the subject and the direction of motion must still read; at full size, finer
  material rewards attention.

## Rhythm

- Separate **primary** motion, **reactive** answers and **ambient** life: different amplitudes, delays and
  rhythms. Do not drive everything with the same sine.
- Alternate preparation, change and a hold. Two beats in 8 s (for example 2 s and 5.6 s) are plenty.
- Silent video: visual events make the rhythm. No BPM sync.
- A loop must agree across the seam in shape, light, texture and speed, not only in the end frame.

## Material: pencil and graphite on warm paper

- Contours with irregular pressure and broken strokes; hatching that follows tone (dense in shadows on
  graphite, in lights on chalk); exposed paper forms the highlights.
- Texture anchored to the picture: it never slides when the surface moves.
- Redraw at 12 phases per second: the structure stays, only fine stroke character changes.
- Palette: graphite and warm paper; any other colour needs a role.

## Review honestly

Defects as `timestamp · object · observation -> exact change -> reinspection`. P0 breaks the render or the
story; P1 is weak staging, poor motion, faulty material or a visible seam; P2 is polish. No "9/10". If you
could not look, write "motion not reviewed".
